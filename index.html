Perspective
AI Product Thinking

How I approach the decisions that matter most when building LLM products.

01
Architecture
RAG before Fine-Tuning

When your data changes fast and iteration speed matters, RAG wins. Fine-tuning is powerful but expensive to maintain. Start with retrieval, layer in fine-tuning only when you've hit its ceiling.

02
Quality
Evaluation is a Product Feature

LLM evaluation isn't a backend concern — it's a product capability. If your team can't measure output quality consistently, you're flying blind. Build evals early, not after launch.

03
Reliability
Fix Context, Not the Model

Retrieval quality and structured feedback loops reduce hallucinations more reliably than swapping models. Most AI quality problems are data and context problems, not model problems.
